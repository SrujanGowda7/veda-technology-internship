# Veda Technology Business & Service Analytics

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of a **4-Day Data Science Internship Project at Veda Technology**.

The project demonstrates how data science can be used to analyze business and service-related data and generate useful insights for decision-making.

Since real company data is private and confidential, a **synthetic dataset** was created to represent realistic business operations.

The project covers website visitors, internship applications, training programs, IT services, digital services, customer inquiries, marketing channels, customer segmentation, business KPIs, and predictive analysis.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project were:

- Create a realistic synthetic business dataset.
- Clean and preprocess business data.
- Analyze website visitor patterns.
- Analyze internship applications.
- Analyze training program performance.
- Analyze IT and digital service demand.
- Analyze customer inquiries and service requests.
- Evaluate marketing-channel performance.
- Calculate important business KPIs.
- Perform customer segmentation.
- Apply predictive machine learning.
- Generate business recommendations from the analysis.

## Dataset

The dataset used in this project is **synthetically generated** and does not contain private or confidential Veda Technology information.

The dataset contains 1,000 simulated business records.

### Main Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer_ID | Unique customer identifier |
| Age | Customer age |
| Gender | Customer gender category |
| Location | Customer location |
| Visitor_Source | Website/marketing source |
| Business_Area | Business area of interaction |
| Service | Requested service |
| Inquiry_Type | Type of inquiry |
| Engagement_Score | Customer engagement score |
| Website_Visits | Number of website visits |
| Applications | Number of applications |
| Training_Hours | Training hours |
| Service_Requests | Number of service requests |
| Satisfaction_Score | Customer satisfaction score |
| Revenue | Simulated revenue |
| Profit | Simulated profit |
| Converted | Conversion indicator |

# Project Structure

The project was completed over four days.

## Day 1 — Data Generation and Data Preparation

### Activities

- Understood the business requirements.
- Identified major business areas.
- Designed a synthetic business dataset.
- Generated customer, visitor, inquiry, service and revenue data.
- Performed initial data inspection.
- Checked data types.
- Checked missing values.
- Checked duplicate records.
- Performed basic preprocessing.

### Outcome

A structured synthetic dataset was created to represent Veda Technology's business and service operations.

---

# Day 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis

### Activities

The second day focused on understanding the patterns present in the dataset.

Analysis included:

- Website visitor analysis.
- Visitor source analysis.
- Internship application analysis.
- Training program analysis.
- IT service analysis.
- Digital service analysis.
- Customer inquiry analysis.
- Service demand analysis.
- Marketing channel analysis.

### Visualizations

Different charts were created using Matplotlib and Seaborn, including:

- Bar charts
- Distribution visualizations
- Service demand charts
- Marketing performance charts
- Business-area comparisons

### Outcome

The analysis helped identify patterns in customer engagement, service demand, applications, inquiries and revenue.

---

# Day 3 — Business Analytics and Customer Segmentation

### Activities

The third day focused on converting the exploratory analysis into business-oriented insights.

### Business KPIs

The following KPIs were calculated:

- Total customers/visitors
- Total applications
- Total inquiries
- Total conversions
- Total service requests
- Total revenue
- Total profit
- Conversion rate
- Profit margin
- Average satisfaction
- Average engagement

### Customer Segmentation

K-Means clustering was used to group customers based on:

- Engagement score
- Website visits
- Service requests
- Satisfaction
- Revenue

StandardScaler was used before applying K-Means.

### Correlation Analysis

A correlation matrix was created to understand relationships between:

- Engagement
- Website visits
- Applications
- Training hours
- Service requests
- Satisfaction
- Revenue
- Profit

### Outcome

The project moved from basic data analysis toward business intelligence and customer-level analysis.

---

# Day 4 — Predictive Analytics and Final Business Insights

### Predictive Analysis

A **Random Forest Regression** model was developed to demonstrate revenue prediction.

The model used business activity features such as:

- Age
- Engagement score
- Website visits
- Applications
- Training hours
- Service requests
- Satisfaction score

### Model Evaluation

The model was evaluated using:

- Mean Absolute Error (MAE)
- Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)
- R² Score

### Feature Importance

Feature importance was also analyzed to understand which variables contributed more to revenue prediction in the simulated dataset.

### Business Recommendations

The final analysis generated recommendations related to:

- High-demand services
- Marketing channels
- Customer engagement
- Business areas
- Revenue generation
- Customer segmentation
- Predictive analytics

- Machine learning results should not be interpreted as real business forecasts.
- The analysis is intended for learning and demonstration purposes.
