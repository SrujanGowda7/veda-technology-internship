# Task 9 – KPI Calculation and Business Metrics

## Veda Technology – Data Science Internship

**Track:** Data Science  
**Task:** Level 1 – Day 9  
**Topic:** KPI Calculation and Business Metrics  
**Tool:** Google Sheets

## Objective

The objective of this task was to understand how raw business data can be converted into measurable Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

## KPIs Calculated

1. Total Revenue – 171,000
2. Total Profit – 34,675
3. Profit Margin – 20.28%
4. Average Order Value – 8,550
5. Total Orders – 20
6. Customer Retention Rate – 30.77%
7. Conversion Rate – 5.52%
8. Revenue Growth Rate – -29.85%

## Methodology

The Sales dataset was analyzed using Google Sheets formulas. Revenue and profit were calculated using SUM, while order count was calculated using COUNTA. Ratios were calculated using clearly defined numerators and denominators.

Customer retention was defined as repeat customers divided by unique customers.

Conversion rate was calculated as total conversions divided by total visitors.

Revenue growth was calculated by comparing revenue from the second period (April–June) with the first period (January–March).

## Key Insights

- Total revenue generated was 171,000.
- Total profit was 34,675.
- Profit margin was 20.28%.
- Average order value was 8,550.
- Customer retention rate was 30.77%.
- Conversion rate was 5.52%.
- Revenue growth was -29.85%, indicating a decline in revenue during the second period.

## Outcome

This task provided practical experience in calculating business KPIs and understanding how analysts convert raw sales data into measurable business performance indicators.
