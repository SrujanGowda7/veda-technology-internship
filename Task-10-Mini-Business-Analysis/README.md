# Task 10 – Mini Business Analysis Report

## Overview

This project analyzes a retail/e-commerce sales dataset and presents business performance insights using key performance indicators (KPIs).

## Business Question

How is the business performing, and what actions can improve revenue, profitability, and customer conversion?

## Objectives

* Analyze overall business performance
* Calculate important business KPIs
* Identify high-performing categories
* Evaluate revenue and profitability
* Identify areas for improvement
* Provide actionable recommendations

## KPIs

| KPI                 |   Result |
| ------------------- | -------: |
| Total Revenue       | ₹171,000 |
| Total Profit        |  ₹34,675 |
| Profit Margin       |   20.28% |
| Average Order Value |   ₹8,550 |
| Total Orders        |       20 |
| Conversion Rate     |    5.52% |
| Revenue Growth Rate |  -29.85% |

## Key Findings

1. Total revenue was ₹171,000.
2. Total profit was ₹34,675.
3. Profit margin was 20.28%.
4. Electronics generated the highest revenue.
5. Clothing generated the highest profit.
6. Conversion rate was 5.52%.
7. Revenue declined by 29.85% in the second period.
8. Improving conversion and revenue growth should be key priorities.

## Recommendations

* Increase targeted promotions for high-revenue categories.
* Focus on profitable products, especially Clothing.
* Improve customer conversion through better offers and customer engagement.
* Investigate the reasons for the revenue decline and implement corrective sales strategies.
