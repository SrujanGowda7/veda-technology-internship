KEY RECOMMENDATIONS
-------------------

1. CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION

Customers are divided into behavioural groups based on
engagement, interests, interactions and spending.

2. TRAINING CUSTOMERS

Customers with high training interest can be targeted
with Data Analytics, Python, AI and Machine Learning
training programs.

3. INTERNSHIP CUSTOMERS

Customers with high internship interest can be targeted
with internship and career development programs.

4. HIGH VALUE CUSTOMERS

Customers with high spending and engagement can be
targeted with premium IT and digital services.

5. OCCASIONAL CUSTOMERS

Occasional customers can be targeted with beginner
training, awareness campaigns and follow-up messages.

6. PERSONALIZED MARKETING

Different customer segments should receive different
marketing campaigns instead of the same offer for everyone.

7. BUSINESS BENEFITS

The system can help improve:

- Customer targeting
- Marketing effectiveness
- Customer engagement
- Service recommendations
- Conversion opportunities
- Revenue opportunities

NOTE:
The dataset is synthetic and created for educational
and internship purposes.
