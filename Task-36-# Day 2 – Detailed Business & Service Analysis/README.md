# Day 2 – Detailed Business & Service Analysis

## Overview

Day 2 focused on deeper analysis of the synthetic Veda Technology business dataset created and cleaned during Day 1.

The analysis covered website traffic, internship applications, training programs, IT and digital services, customer inquiries, marketing channels, service demand, customer groups, locations and monthly business performance.

## Analysis Performed

The following areas were analyzed:

- Website visitor activity
- Internship application patterns
- Training program performance
- IT service inquiries
- Digital service inquiries
- Customer and service requests
- Marketing channel performance
- Business area performance
- Service demand
- Location performance
- Customer type performance
- Response time
- Customer satisfaction
- Monthly business performance

## Key Business Findings

### 1. Marketing Channel Performance

College Referral achieved the highest simulated conversion rate at **34.48%**.

This indicates that referrals from colleges performed strongly in converting visitors within the synthetic dataset.

### 2. Service Demand

**Data Analytics Training** recorded the highest demand with **143 records**.

This made it the most requested service in the simulated dataset.

### 3. Service Revenue

**Digital Marketing** generated the highest simulated service-level revenue at **₹1,134,155**.

### 4. Business Area Performance

**Internship** was the business area generating the highest total simulated revenue.

### 5. Location Performance

**Pune** recorded the highest number of conversions among the locations analyzed.
