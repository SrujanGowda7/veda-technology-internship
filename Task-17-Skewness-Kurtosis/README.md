## Key Concepts

### Skewness

Skewness measures the asymmetry of a data distribution.

* Skewness close to 0: Approximately symmetric
* Positive skewness: Right-skewed distribution
* Negative skewness: Left-skewed distribution
* Large absolute skewness: Highly asymmetric distribution

### Kurtosis

Kurtosis describes the tail heaviness of a distribution.

* Positive kurtosis: Heavier tails
* Negative kurtosis: Lighter tails
* Kurtosis close to 0: Approximately normal-tailed distribution
