# Task 19 – Independent Samples t-Test

## Objective
To compare the means of two independent groups and determine whether the difference between their average values is statistically significant.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- SciPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

## Tasks Performed
- Created two independent groups of sample data.
- Calculated the mean and standard deviation of both groups.
- Inspected the distributions using a histogram.
- Formulated null and alternative hypotheses.
- Performed an independent samples t-test.
- Calculated the t-statistic and p-value.
- Interpreted statistical significance using a significance level of 0.05.

## Hypotheses

**Null Hypothesis (H0):** The mean of Group A is equal to the mean of Group B.

**Alternative Hypothesis (H1):** The mean of Group A is not equal to the mean of Group B.

## Conclusion
The independent samples t-test was used to determine whether the difference between the means of two independent groups was statistically significant. The p-value was compared with the significance level of 0.05 to make the final decision.

A p-value below 0.05 indicates a statistically significant difference between the groups, while a p-value equal to or above 0.05 indicates insufficient evidence to conclude that the group means are different.
